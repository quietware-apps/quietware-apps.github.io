Common questions

How does the stair layout work?

Set the total rise, floor to floor. The app uses the fewest risers that keep each one at or under the tallest riser you allow (7 3/4" by default), then lays out the treads, the run and the pencil marks along the stringer's top edge. Each mark is worked out from the start, not added step by step, so the top mark never drifts.

What is the bottom riser drop?

The bottom floor gets no tread, but every other step does, so the stringer is cut one tread thickness shorter at the bottom. The app shows that cut under the tread thickness box.

Are the code checks official?

No. The green and red badges use typical US residential limits (riser 7 3/4" or less, tread 10" or more, a rafter notch no deeper than a quarter of its depth, 1 1/2" of bearing). They are a guide only. Check your local code before you cut.

Can I use millimetres?

Yes. Tap FT-IN | MM at the top of any tool. Every box and answer switches, and anything you typed is converted exactly.
