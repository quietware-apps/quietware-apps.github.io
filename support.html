Common questions

How do I get a recipe in?

On the Recipes screen: Read screenshots (up to 5, in any order), Use the camera for a cookbook page or recipe card, or Paste text you copied anywhere. Long Instagram captions often need two or three screenshots; the app puts them back in order and removes the lines they share.

Some text from my screenshot is missing.

The app keeps the recipe and sets aside the rest of the screen: likes, buttons, banners, video subtitles. Open "Other text in the pictures" at the bottom of the recipe to see what was set aside.

How does the pan math work?

Each kind of pan is filled to its usual level (a cake pan about half way, a muffin cup two thirds), so the app compares the batter each pan takes. That is why a 9x13 recipe comes out at about 24 cupcakes. Baking times are not calculated; the app tells you which way the time moves and when to start checking.

Why does an amount say "close match"?

Conversions between cups and grams use each ingredient's own weight per cup, from the public USDA FoodData Central. When the app only has a near relative (skyr counted as Greek yogurt, for example), it says so.

What does the free version include?

Reading, scaling, pans and cooking, and up to 10 saved recipes. One payment saves any number of recipes and adds custom pan sizes. There is no subscription, and later updates are included.

I bought the unlock. How do I restore it?

Open Settings → Restore purchase, or tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Does the app need the internet?

No. Reading, scaling and cooking all work offline. Only the purchase and Restore purchase talk to the App Store.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
