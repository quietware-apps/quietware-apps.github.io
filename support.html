Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my log to a new iPhone?

Your iPhone's backup includes the app's data. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup and your catches, tackle and photos come with it. For an extra copy, use Settings → Export to save CSV files of your catches and tackle.

What does the free version include?

Everything, up to 10 catches and 10 tackle items. One payment removes the limits. There is no subscription. Export is always free.

I bought the unlock. How do I restore it?

Open Settings → Restore purchase, or tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Can I enter weight in decimal pounds or kilograms?

Yes. Settings → Units: pounds and ounces, decimal pounds, or metric. Sizes you already logged convert automatically.

Does the app use GPS or show maps?

No. Spots are names you type, so they never leave your phone and never show up on anyone's map.

I deleted a lure. What happened to its catches?

The catches stay in your log; they just no longer name that lure. To keep the history, mark tackle as "lost or retired" instead of deleting it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
