Tin Math

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Short version: your recipes, pictures and notes stay on your iPhone. Text in your pictures is read on the iPhone itself and never uploaded. There is no account, no cloud, no tracking, no ads and no analytics. The only thing that leaves your phone is an anonymous record of the one-time purchase, so the unlock can be confirmed.

2. Data that stays on your iPhone

Everything you add is stored only in the app's storage on your device:

  • Recipes you read from screenshots or photos, or paste as text, and the text the app set aside from them.
  • A copy of the screenshots or photo each recipe was read from, and the photo of your dish if you add one.
  • Names, notes, the last scale you used, and the pan sizes you add.
  • Settings such as appearance.

Pictures you pick or take are read on the iPhone with Apple's built-in text recognition and are not uploaded anywhere. We never receive any of this data. The app has no server and no account, and it does not use your location.

Your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup, if you have it turned on) includes the app's data, so it can move with you to a new phone. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

3. The one-time purchase

The optional "Unlock forever" purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details.

To confirm that the unlock is valid, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a purchase-verification service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and the purchase record (product, date, store, country and currency). This record is not linked to your identity, is not used for tracking, and is not combined with any other data. It is used only so the app knows whether you have unlocked it, including when you tap Restore purchase. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

4. What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No uploading of your pictures or recipes, and no AI services.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No location access.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

6. Deleting

Deleting a recipe removes it from your phone. Recent keeps your last 20 unsaved recipes and removes older ones by itself. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone. The anonymous purchase record described in section 3 remains with Apple and RevenueCat so you can restore your unlock.

7. Your rights

If you are in the EU, UK or another region with data protection rights, you can ask about, access or delete the purchase record linked to the anonymous ID by emailing us. Because we hold no other data about you, there is nothing else to provide. The legal basis for the purchase record is performance of the contract you entered by buying the unlock. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Ireland that is the Data Protection Commission.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
